Privacy

Caliper, by Warren Labs. Last updated 18 September 2026.

Caliper collects nothing and sends nothing. Your measurements are files on your own device. That is not a promise about our intentions. It is a property of how the app is built, and you can check it.

Why you can check it rather than trust it

  • The Mac App Store build requests no network entitlement at all. Its entitlements file contains three things (the app sandbox, audio input, and read/write access to files you pick in a system panel) and nothing else. An app without com.apple.security.network.client cannot open an outbound connection. The sandbox refuses it.
  • There is no analytics library, no crash reporter and no telemetry of any kind.
  • The interface is native, not a web view. There is no embedded browser that could fetch anything on its own.

A later version will be able to submit a measurement

It is built and it is not in 1.0, because the service it uploads to does not exist yet. When it ships it will need the network entitlement, and this page will change in the same release, never after it.

When it does: it will send only the measurement bundle: the curves, the impulse responses behind them, the rig and calibration serials, and the session metadata you typed. It will show you that summary before sending, nothing will be sent automatically, and a submitted measurement will be published under a non-commercial licence with attribution, with you keeping your copyright.

What Caliper does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No collection of usage data, device identifiers, or location.
  • No automatic upload of measurements. If you share a measurement, you do it deliberately, with the system share sheet, to a destination you choose.

Sharing is yours, and it ends our involvement

Caliper can export a measurement bundle or a graphic. Once you hand that to another app, a message, or a website, that destination's privacy policy governs it and ours no longer applies. We never see it and have no way to.

Children

Caliper is a measurement instrument for audio work. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, the most likely change is a new capability, and if one ever transmits anything, it will say so plainly here before it ships, not after.
