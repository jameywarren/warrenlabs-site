Our take

The Edition 8 pairs a bright, detailed top with a strong low end and Ultrasone’s angled-driver “S-Logic” staging, in a small closed body meant to travel. It is a V-shaped luxury tuning more than a neutral one: energetic and isolating, with treble that EQs down easily if it is sharp.

Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones our measurements support.

Does the Ultrasone Edition 8 need an amp?

No: sensitive and easy to drive from portable sources, which is the point of the design.

Is the Edition 8 neutral?

No, it is a bright, bassy luxury tuning. Fun and detailed rather than reference-flat.
