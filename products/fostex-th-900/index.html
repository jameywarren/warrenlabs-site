Our take

The TH900 is unapologetically exciting: deep, powerful, well-textured bass and a sparkling, sometimes sharp treble around clean mids, a dramatic V-shape done at flagship quality. Easy to drive but scales with a good source. EQ the treble peak if it bites; the bass is the whole point.

Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones our measurements support.

Does the Fostex TH900 need an amp?

It is easy to drive but benefits from a clean, capable source to control that big bass.

Is the TH900 bassy?

Very: deep and powerful, but textured rather than bloated. The treble is bright to match.
