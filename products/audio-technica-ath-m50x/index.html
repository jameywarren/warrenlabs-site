Our take

The M50x is a closed, isolating monitor with boosted bass and an energetic upper treble, a V-shape that reads as exciting more than neutral. Great for tracking and noisy rooms; if you want it flatter, a little EQ pulls the bass and treble back in line.

Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones our measurements support.

Does the ATH-M50x need an amp?

No. 38 Ω and sensitive, it is happy off a phone or an interface headphone output.

Is the M50x good for mixing?

For tracking and reference in loud spaces, yes. For final mix decisions its V-shape means you will want a flatter check or some EQ.

Is the M50x closed or open?

Closed-back. It isolates well, which is why it is everywhere in studios and on commutes.
