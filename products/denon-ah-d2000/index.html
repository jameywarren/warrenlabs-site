Our take

The D2000 is the affordable Denon bio-cellulose closed-back: satisfying deep bass, a smooth non-fatiguing top, and light weight. It gives up a little refinement to the D5000/7000 but keeps the fun, and it’s a classic base for wood-cup mods. Easy to drive, long discontinued.

Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones our measurements support.

Does the Denon AH-D2000 need an amp?

No: sensitive and low-impedance, it is easy to drive well.

D2000 vs D5000 vs D7000?

Ascending refinement and price; same warm, fun closed signature. The D2000 is the value pick.
