Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones our measurements support.

HD 600 vs HD 650?

Close cousins. The 600 is a touch brighter and more forward; the 650 is warmer with a gentler top. Neither is “better”: pick the balance you prefer.

Does the HD 600 need an amp?

Benefits from one, does not demand it. 300 Ω wants voltage; a good dongle or any real headphone output is enough for honest levels.

Is the HD 600 good for mixing?

Yes. It is a longtime neutral studio reference for tonal balance. Check the very top end on a second source.
