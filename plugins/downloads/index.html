After you buy

Downloads and installation.

Two things arrive: the plug-ins, which you download here, and your licence files, which come by email. You need both.

1 · Your licence file

One file arrives by email within a few minutes of paying — Warren Labs.wllicense if you bought a bundle, or <Plugin>.wllicense for a single plug-in. One file covers everything you bought.

Drag it onto any plug-in window. That unlocks your whole purchase — you do not need to do it once per plug-in. The ⓘ panel will then read Licensed to your name.

Prefer not to drag? Open the ⓘ panel and use Install on the LICENCE row. Either way, the file itself is filed under ~/Music/Warren Labs/, which is where the plug-ins look for it.

Nothing after ten minutes? Check spam, then email jamey@warrenlabs.com and we will send it straight over. Refunds within 14 days, same address.

3 · Installing

Level and Pare are signed and notarized by Apple: unzip and drag, no warning. The rest are unsigned betas — macOS shows a one-time “unidentified developer” warning on first open. It is safe to allow:

  • macOS 15 and later: double-click the .pkg. When macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  • macOS 14 and earlier: right-click the .pkg → Open → Open.

Signed builds are replacing the betas plug-in by plug-in. Every update through 1.0 is included in what you paid.

.component → ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
.vst3 → ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
.app → /Applications

Relaunch your DAW afterwards so it rescans. If a plug-in does not appear, that rescan is almost always the reason.
