01 · PICK YOUR HEADPHONE

Every headphone in Attune was measured on my own rig. If yours isn’t there yet, you can import a measurement you already have (CSV, AutoEq or squig) for free, or send your pair in and I’ll measure it.

02 · ANSWER A OR B

About a dozen short comparisons, blind and loudness matched, so louder can never pass for better. It crossfades between the two instead of muting, so the only thing that changes is the sound.

03 · KEEP IT

Your answers build one curve, saved per headphone. One saved sound is free and stays free. Pro is a one-time $39 unlock for unlimited sounds and AutoEq export. It is not a subscription and never will be.

EQ moves tonal balance and nothing else. It won’t buy you resolution, soundstage, lower distortion or different driver physics, and nothing on this page says otherwise.

THE BENCH · LIVINGSTON, MONTANA

Why trust the measurements

Because I wrote the method down and you can check it. A measurement you can’t audit is just a picture of a line.

FIXTUREminiDSP EARS Pro, IEC 60318-4
INSTRUMENT FLOOR0.002 dB, 200 Hz – 2 kHz
PUBLISHEDRaw, full res, CC BY-NC 4.0
BENCHLivingston, Montana
  1. The rig.

    A miniDSP EARS Pro with per-unit serial-matched calibration, using the IEC 60318-4 couplers that the industry rigs use. It is a house reference rather than an industry reference coupler, and I label it that way next to every graph.

  2. Seated for best fit, deliberately.

    I adjust the headphone for seal and for sound, the way you would settle in to listen, rather than dropping it on at random. You don’t listen at a random seating, so I measure the good one and then show I can find it again.

  3. Published raw, flattering or not.

    Every measurement I take goes up at full resolution under CC BY-NC 4.0, with the rig and the date on it. See the measurements.
