04 · WHO I AM

25 years in audio.

First employee at Grace Design, building high-end microphone preamps and headphone amplifiers still used in studios worldwide.

Fourteen years at HeadRoom Corporation, from product management through President and CEO. Full P&L. Designed and launched the Coda and Overture amplifier and DAC products. Built and ran the dealer network alongside direct sales.

Self-funded headphone R&D through to three working prototypes with even response and extended low end. Consulted Menlo Scientific on the design. Traveled to Japanese headphone shows and Philippine manufacturing facilities to evaluate production partners.

Author of The Art and Science of Headphone Design.

Currently building Warren Labs: measurement, correction software, and makerphones, an open-source 3D-printable headphone project.

05 · WHY BRING IN AN OUTSIDER

Closeness is the problem.

Product teams get close to a design. That closeness is what makes the product good, and it is also what makes the last few decisions hard to see.

I am not going to tell you what your product should be. I am going to tell you what it currently is, in terms you can act on, from someone who has been on the other side of the same decisions.

06 · PRODUCTION MEASUREMENT

Before a run ships, or after one goes wrong.

Send us a sample from your production units. We measure every one on the same rig under one documented seating protocol, and report unit-to-unit and channel-to-channel variance against your design intent. You get the report, the graphs, and the raw data.

I ran hardware test and QC on a factory floor before I ran the company.
