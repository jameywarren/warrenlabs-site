What you get

Four reasons worth the shipping.

Questions

The obvious ones.

Does it cost me anything?

No. We email you a prepaid, insured return label and cover shipping both ways. If you would rather not pay to ship it to us either, ask — we prepay inbound for local and community contributors.

Is my headphone insured in transit?

Yes, both directions. You tell us the value, we insure to it, and we photograph the unit on arrival and again before it ships back — documented, not a gotcha.

How long will you keep it?

We measure and ship it back within about five business days of arrival. If anything will take longer, we tell you before you send it.

What if the measurement is unflattering?

We publish it anyway. Honest graphs are the whole point — we show exactly how a headphone measures, good or bad. What we will not do is dress it up.

What if you can’t publish mine?

Sometimes a unit arrives damaged, won’t seal on the rig, or gives data we don’t trust. If that happens we tell you why, you still get your unit’s curve, and we simply don’t publish it.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to send one in. Points, your level, and your on-page credit ride on a free community profile, so making one is just how they attach to you — the measurement and your curve are yours either way.

What headphones can you measure?

Over-ear and on-ear headphones, and IEMs where the rig takes a proper seal. If you’re not sure yours qualifies, tell us in the form and we’ll say.

How is this different from the historical Archive?

The Archive’s older curves were measured on a Head Acoustics HMS II.3. Your headphone is measured here on our own rig (a miniDSP EARS Pro) and labeled as such. We don’t silently mix the two — they’re different measurement systems, especially up in the treble.
